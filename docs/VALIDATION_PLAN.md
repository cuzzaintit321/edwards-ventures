# First paid pilot — validation plan

## Narrow offer
AI-assisted lead-response workflow for one local service business, with **human review before sending**. It takes inbound inquiry text and produces a suggested response for staff to approve. Do not promise automatic integrations not yet built.

## Buyer
Small service business receiving inbound requests, with one owner or office manager managing replies.

## Initial interview questions
1. How do inquiries currently arrive and get answered?
2. How many requests come in during a typical week?
3. Which types of replies are repetitive?
4. What happens when messages are delayed?
5. Who would approve AI-generated replies?
6. Would a limited, paid trial be useful? What outcome would justify the fee?

## Offer hypothesis
Offer one scoped pilot at a **test price** of $300 setup and $75 for 30 days of support; adjust based on actual buyer conversations. Do not make revenue claims.

## Success signals
- At least 10 interviews with target buyers.
- 3 agree to see a demo.
- 1 signs a written paid pilot agreement.
- Approval and response-time metrics measured during the pilot.

## Guardrails
- Do not use real client data in the public demo.
- No unsolicited automated emails or texts.
- No confidential data sent to third-party AI providers without suitable agreements, notices, and controls.
- Clarify data retention and delete-on-request procedures before production use.

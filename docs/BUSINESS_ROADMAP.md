# 30-day business roadmap

**Week 1:** Choose name as provisional; check relevant trademarks/business records/domain; define target niche and one offer; prepare demo using invented data.

**Week 2:** Speak with 10 business owners or operators and record what they actually need; avoid building based on guesses.

**Week 3:** Create one clear proposal and small paid pilot; arrange appropriate registration, tax and banking steps before commercial operations as required.

**Week 4:** Deliver pilot, get feedback and a testimonial only with permission; document actual metrics and decide whether to improve, pivot, or stop.

## Structure
A single operating company can own multiple products/brands, subject to legal/tax advice. Starting with separate LLCs for every prototype is not necessary. Company structure, assumed-name filings, insurance, and contracts depend on the actual activities and jurisdiction.
